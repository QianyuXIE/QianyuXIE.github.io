# Third-party artwork

`textures/chungking-express.jpg` is a reduced-size, uncropped copy of the official
Chungking Express theatrical artwork made available in the Janus Films press downloads.
It is also embedded in `qianyu-room.glb` as the wall-poster texture.

- Film/source page: https://www.janusfilms.com/films/1030
- Original image: https://s3.amazonaws.com/criterion-production/janus_promo_packages/451-/WKW_Poster_ChungkingExpress_original.jpg
- Retrieved: 2026-09-09
- Film: Chungking Express (1994), directed by Wong Kar Wai.
