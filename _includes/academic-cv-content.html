About Me

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (AI concentration) at the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

I received my B.S. in Electronic Information Science and Technology from Jilin University. My current research focuses on multimodal large language models, cross-modal affective computing, and efficient generative models. I am also an AI Model Engineer Intern at Huawei's 2012 Laboratories, where I work on scalable image generation with Mixture-of-Experts, recurrent computation, and Diffusion Transformers.
